## 2 · The Product

**The problem:** Small businesses manage customers, appointments, tasks, and notes across too many disconnected tools, which makes follow-up and organization harder than it should be.

**Who it's for:** Small businesses and service-based teams.

**Three core features (the MVP):**

1. Customer management: store customer profiles, contact details, notes, and interaction history.
2. Appointments and tasks: schedule work and assign follow-up tasks to the team.
3. Team workspace: share customer information securely with role-based access.

**What ships by Week 13 (demo day):** A working web app that allows a small team to manage customer records, schedule appointments, track tasks, and collaborate with role-based access without major bugs.

**Out of scope / v2 ideas (Week-9 pitch fodder):** Advanced analytics, billing, inventory, and multi-tenant SaaS features.

## 4 · Code & Review Norms

**Branch & PR flow:** One issue leads to one branch and one pull request. All PRs must be reviewed and pass required checks before merge.

**What blocks approval:** Failing checks, unclear or unsafe code, missing tests, broken preview, or unanswered review comments.

**Review response time:** Within 24 hours on weekdays and 48 hours on weekends.

**Comment conventions:**
- `nit:` optional improvement
- `q:` clarification question
- `blocker:` must be fixed before merge

## 5 · AI Working Norms

**Course policy (not optional):** No AI-generated code is merged without human review. The PR author owns every line they submit and verifies any AI-assisted work by running the code.

**How we use AI as a team:** We use AI to brainstorm, clarify ideas, and draft code, but we still test and understand everything before merging.

**What we never delegate to AI:** We do not let AI decide architecture, final code quality, or review decisions without human verification.

## 6 · When Things Go Wrong

Stuck protocol (course default): 15 minutes stuck → post in the team thread → still stuck at stand-up → TA → office hours.

**If someone can't deliver on time:** Tell the team as soon as possible and propose a revised plan, handoff, or timeline.

**If we have a conflict:** Raise it at stand-up. If it continues, escalate to the TA and then the instructor if needed.
